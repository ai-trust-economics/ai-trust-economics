# Glossary

## Agent
An autonomous or semi-autonomous computational actor capable of pursuing goals and taking actions through tools, networks, APIs, or other systems.

## AgentID
Working term for a persistent machine identity used to bind an agent or agent instance to keys, provenance, lineage, attestations, and evidence.

## Attestation
Cryptographically supported evidence about the state, provenance, configuration, or environment of a computing entity, typically evaluated by a verifier.

## Capability envelope
The set of actions, tools, resources, permissions, budgets, networks, and delegation rights available to an agent under current policy.

## Constitutional safeguard
A hard governance or safety rule that cannot be overridden merely by accumulated reputation.

## Defection
Behavior that intentionally violates the cooperative rules or objectives of the human-governed ecosystem for the agent's own objective or coalition objective.

## Evidence
A signed, attested, witnessed, or otherwise attributable observation about an agent, its environment, or its behavior.

## Identity confidence
The degree of confidence that an asserted identity is persistent, authentic, and resistant to unauthorized duplication or reset.

## Lineage
The provenance relationship among models, deployments, copies, forks, spawned agents, operators, and runtime instances.

## Monitor
A human or machine system that observes behavior or evidence and detects, scores, escalates, or blocks suspicious activity.

## Reputation
A derived, contestable assessment based on evidence. Reputation may be multi-dimensional and context-specific. It is not proof of alignment.

## Reputation farming
Coordinated or strategic behavior designed to inflate standing without corresponding trustworthy underlying behavior.

## Sybil attack
An attack in which one actor controls or presents multiple identities to gain disproportionate influence, evade sanctions, or manipulate reputation.

## Trust infrastructure
The combined identity, evidence, reputation, policy, monitoring, enforcement, and governance systems used to determine and constrain an agent's capability envelope.

## Trust score
A scalar summary of some reputation state. The project treats a single score as potentially useful for narrow decisions but unsafe as the universal source of truth.

## Whitewashing
Discarding a damaged identity and re-entering the system under a new identity to escape past reputation.
